# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone,
regardless of experience, background, identity or opinion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Give helpful, specific feedback.
- Respect privacy. Never post personal data, credentials or private energy data.
- Do not use the project to harm people or infrastructure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately unsafe or malicious energy control changes.
- Spam or repeated off-topic demands.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Report concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer
contact. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
